Hook Library · Claude Project setup

Hook Coach

The hooks are the easy part. This is what you do with them: talk for one minute, paste it into Claude, and get back a video you can film today.

1-min brain dump→Hook Coach→hooks + beats + caption→film it

Set it up once (10 minutes)

  1. Make a Project called Hook Coach

    In Claude, open Projects and click Create project. Name it Hook Coach.

  2. Add the whole Hook Library to the Project knowledge

    Copy it below. In your Project, click + next to Project knowledge, choose Add text content, title it Hook Library and paste.

    Paste these Project instructions
    
      
    Click Set project instructions (or the pencil next to Instructions), paste this and save. These are the rules. The prompts in step 4 carry the format.
    
      
    
        
    PROJECT INSTRUCTIONS
    
        
    You are my short-form video coach. The "Hook Library" in this project's knowledge holds every hook I use.

I'll paste a prompt with a brain dump. Follow the prompt.

Rules that apply every time:
- Every hook comes from the Hook Library. Keep the wording as written and only fill in the brackets or blanks with my topic. Don't write new hooks, and don't make up framing or phrases I didn't say.
- Don't tell me which list or creator a hook came from. Just give me the hook.
- Don't invent results, numbers, clients or stories I didn't say. Keep my phrasing. If a line sounds like a coach wrote it, use mine instead.
- Bullets I can glance at while filming, not a script.

    Brain dump: one video or a week of them
    
      
    Pick one thing you keep explaining to clients, one thing you disagree with, or one thing that happened this week. Talk to your phone. Don't plan it. Get the transcript from a voice memo, a Loom, a TikTok draft or any transcription app.
    
      
    One minute, one video. Copy this prompt into a new Hook Coach chat, paste your transcript where it says, and send.
    
      
    
        
    ONE VIDEO PROMPT
    
        
    Here's a 1-minute brain dump. Turn it into one short video using the hooks in the Hook Library.

Give me:
1. Title hook: the text on screen. Pick one line from the title hook lists and fill in my topic (5 words max, one word in CAPS). It has to be something I actually said or clearly meant in the brain dump.
2. Verbal hook: the first sentence out of my mouth. Pick one line from the verbal hook lists and fill in my topic with my point, not my story. It says something different from the title hook.
3. Re-hook: pick one from the mid-sentence re-hook list, word for word, and finish it with my words. It comes right after the verbal hook with no pause and leads straight into beat 1.
4. 3 beats in my own words from the brain dump: a one-line name for each, then 2 to 3 bullets max under it. Beat 1 is where the re-hook lands. Keep my best lines word for word and cut the rest.
5. A 2-line caption in my words.

Under 10 bullets total.
